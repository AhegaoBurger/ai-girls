# Pitch Deck Outline (flesh out after validation data lands)

**Working codename:** Tonari (NAME: TBD)
**Use:** investor / partner narrative. Best assembled *after* the landing page
produces real willingness-to-pay numbers — those become Slide 8.

Narrative arc: **consumer wedge → embodiment platform → ambient-AI-scale endgame.**

1. **Hook / vision** — "AI is about to have a body. We're building the layer that
   gives it one." One unforgettable line + the avatar, alive.

2. **Problem** — AI companionship is exploding but flat: text bubbles and 2D images.
   No presence. Loneliness is a massive, growing market; current products don't feel
   *there*.

3. **Solution** — Tonari: a 3D companion with real-time voice and **adaptive,
   non-scripted movement** that shares your space. Demo/GIF: movie night, a date.

4. **Why now** — Real-time voice LLMs (OpenAI Realtime / Gemini Live), text-to-motion
   research maturing, cheap 3D on the web, AR glasses on the horizon. The pieces just
   arrived.

5. **The moat / "secret"** — Open-ended embodied motion is **not solved, even
   academically** (cite the research: MotionLCM, MoMADiff, ACTOR — all lab-stage).
   Whoever ships reliable "drop in an agent → adaptive body + voice" owns
   infrastructure. Plus a data flywheel: every interaction trains better motion.

6. **Product / how it works** — engine-agnostic "brain" (LLM + voice + memory)
   driving a swappable renderer over a motion-intent protocol; web/mobile now,
   AR-ready via open standards (glTF/VRM, WebXR). (Trim technical depth to taste.)

7. **Market** — Consumer AI companion TAM (Replika, Character.AI, AIKO, Yume, etc. —
   proof it's hot) → expand to the platform TAM: any agent/brand/game that wants an
   embodied front-end.

8. **Traction / validation** — *(fill from the landing-page test)*: visitors, paid-
   intent conversion %, tier split, founding-member signups, qualitative use-cases,
   CAC by channel. This slide is why we run the page first.

9. **Business model** — Paid subscription from day one (~$15 / ~$29 tiers), no free
   tier — strong margins and proven willingness to pay. Then platform/API pricing.

10. **The big endgame** — Ambient, embodied AI everywhere + **opt-in, native,
    context-aware monetization at scale** (the "ad" upside, framed responsibly). This
    is the "and then it gets huge" slide.

11. **Competition / why we win** — Everyone else is chat + canned-animation avatars.
    We win on adaptive embodiment + platform + data flywheel. 2x2 or feature matrix.

12. **Team / ask** — Who you are, why you, what you're raising and what it unlocks
    (build the motion engine + ship the consumer app + gather the data).

> Keep it visual and emotional — this is a product people *feel*. Lead with the
> avatar and the demo, not the tech stack.
